Privacy Policy

App: KOTODAMA  ·  Last updated: October 30, 2026  ·  Platform: iOS

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains the information collected and used by KOTODAMA ("the App"). Please read this policy carefully before using the App. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

This policy applies to the iOS version of the App.

Information We Collect

The App does not require account registration. We do not collect or store any information that identifies you.

1. On-Device Content (Words & Voice)

  • Affirmation script text
  • Voice recordings made by the user

All of this data is stored only on your device and is never transmitted outside the App (to any server or cloud).

2. Microphone

The App requests microphone access for the voice recording feature. Recorded audio is used only on your device and, as noted above, is never transmitted externally.

3. Advertising Information (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob to display ads. Ads are served on a non-personalized basis only (not based on your behavioral history); we do not collect identifiers (such as IDFA) used for personalized advertising. For this reason, the App does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission. Depending on your region, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) may be shown before ads are displayed.

4. Purchase Information (Apple In-App Purchase)

In-app purchases (one-time, non-consumable) in the App are processed through Apple's App Store. Payment details such as credit card numbers are processed directly by Apple's platform; the App does not collect or store them.

How We Use Your Information

  • Providing the App's core features (affirmation playback, recording, and script management)
  • Delivering ads (non-personalized)
  • Processing in-app purchases
  • Improving the App and investigating issues

Third-Party Sharing

The App uses the following service providers. Each provider manages information according to its own privacy policy.

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC): Ad delivery (non-personalized)
    Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple Inc.: In-app purchase payment processing
    Apple Privacy Policy

We do not sell or rent personal information to any other third parties. However, we may disclose information as required by law.

Data Storage

Usage data such as words and voice recordings is stored only on your device. The App does not operate its own servers to store this data.

Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that such information has been collected, we will promptly delete it.
